Our Origins

How Stackly Began

Founded in 2020 by a team of software engineers and master mechanics, Stackly was born out of frustration with the opaque and outdated traditional auto repair industry.

We realized that cars were evolving into supercomputers on wheels, yet the way they were serviced hadn't changed in decades. Stackly integrates proprietary diagnostic AI with hands-on mechanical excellence.

Our Mission

To provide transparent, high-tech, and reliable automotive services that empower drivers and prolong vehicle lifespans.

Our Vision

To be the global standard for smart garage solutions, seamlessly blending automotive mechanical engineering with cutting-edge software.
